Twin Screw Pumps

Twin Screw Pumps

Gear-timed, dual-flow twin screw pumps: two left- and right-handed screw spindles rotate in a closed compartment, drawing liquid toward the ends and propelling it axially toward the centre. This dual flow nullifies axial thrust completely, giving a long, trouble-free service life with no metal-to-metal contact and the ability to run dry for limited periods. Conforms to API 676, 2nd edition.

Extended Life

Wear Compensation Stator

The progressive cavity pump is well established as the pump of choice for handling abrasive solids. Wear optimisation is achieved through geometric design for minimum internal fluid velocities and by selecting the proper speed and material grade of rotor and stator. Wear-compensation stator design lets Roto Pumps control and regulate volumetric efficiency for an extended period even as components wear.
