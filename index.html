Who we are

A pioneer in Positive Displacement Pumps

We are pioneers in manufacturing Positive Displacement Pumps, ranking among the top 10 global players. With an integrated, state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, we continuously endeavor to increase our product portfolio to cater to a wider spectrum of industrial pumps and cover as many applications as possible.

Our core strength lies in focused R&D, robust marketing, and a sales network spanning 5 continents. Our plants are ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and OHSAS 18001 certified.

Extended service life

Wear Compensation Stator

The progressive cavity pump is well established as the pump of choice for handling abrasive solids. Our wear-compensation stator design allows Roto Pumps to control and regulate volumetric efficiency over an extended service life — even as rotor and stator wear.
