Home / Industries

25+ industries, one rotor-and-stator principle

We take pride in our ability to efficiently handle over 5,000 fluids across more than 25 industry sectors around the world — matched to the right pump, elastomer and metallurgy for each one.

Mining & Explosives

Cement grout, coal-water mixtures, mine dewatering, explosive-mix transfer and rock-dust slurries handled by our abrasion-tolerant wide-throat and standard pumps.

Sugar Industry

From raw cane juice with fibre to massecuite, molasses and press mud — every stage of sugar processing, in standard and wide-throat configurations.

Paint, Varnish & Ink

Pigmented paints, lacquers, resins and dispersion coatings pumped without shearing or foaming the media.

Wastewater Treatment

Activated sludge, dewatered cakes, digested sludge and chemical dosing across municipal and industrial treatment plants.

Oil & Gas Industry

Crude transfer, drilling mud, produced water, polymer flooding and multiphase fluid transfer onshore and offshore.

Pulp & Paper Industry

Black liquor, coating clays, cellulose slurries and paper sludge across the full pulp-and-paper process.

Chemical Industry

Acids, alkalis, resins and specialty chemicals — handled with the right elastomer and metallurgy for each fluid.

Food & Beverage

Purees, sauces, dairy, confectionery and viscous food pastes moved gently with hygienic, CIP-ready pumps.

Marine & Offshore

Bilge, ballast water, fuel oil, sludge and lubricating oil handled aboard vessels and offshore platforms.

Bio Gas Industry

Organic slurries and digestate feed for biogas plants, engineered around variable-consistency biomass.
