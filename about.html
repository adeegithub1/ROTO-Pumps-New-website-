A globally preferred name in positive displacement pumps

Established in 1968, Roto Pumps is the pioneer manufacturer of Progressive Cavity Pumps in India — a public-listed company with presence across five continents and a legacy of more than 55 years.

Company overview

Built on an in-house engineering philosophy

Roto Pumps provides efficient, reliable pumping solutions to a diverse range of industries including Wastewater, Sugar, Paper, Paint, Oil & Gas, Chemicals & Process, Ceramics, Food & Beverages, Renewable Energy & Power, Mining & Explosives, and Marine.

With a state-of-the-art manufacturing unit in Greater Noida and an ultra-modern R&D centre in Noida, Roto Pumps successfully exports to more than 50 countries — and is ambitiously expanding strategic global partnerships toward a target of 100+ countries.

The people behind the pump

A genius ahead of his times

In 1968, having used progressive cavity pumps extensively in his own coal mine, Mr. Gupta invented a unique manufacturing process for machining the intricate profiled rotor — the heart of the progressive cavity pump. This gave birth to Roto Pumps.

Mr. Ram Ratan Gupta
04.02.1918 – 14.02.1980 · Founder
